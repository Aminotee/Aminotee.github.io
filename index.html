01 — About

Music for the room

Aminote is a pianist playing for hotel lounges, bars, restaurants and private functions. The set list is built around what a room actually wants to hear while it eats, talks and relaxes: warm jazz standards, familiar sentimental pieces and smooth lounge material — plus Andalusian and Arabic-flavored pieces that most touring lounge pianists don't carry, giving venues something locally distinctive.
