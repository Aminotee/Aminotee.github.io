About

Music that turns a room
into a moment

I'm Amine, a pianist specialising in hotel lounge and dinner performance — the art of reading a room and giving it exactly the atmosphere it needs. An intimate ballad while couples dine. A bossa nova as the evening warms up. A standard everyone recognises within two bars.

My repertoire spans over 80 pieces across five worlds: classic jazz standards, sentimental favourites, modern lounge, and — what makes my performances unlike anyone else's — the Andalusian and Arabic songbook, played with a jazz pianist's touch.

Signature

Two traditions,
one piano

Most lounge pianists play the standards. Very few can follow "Misty" with "Lamma Bada Yatathanna", or slip from Cole Porter into a Hijaz improvisation. This is the sound of Morocco meeting Manhattan — and it stops people mid-conversation.

I

The American Songbook

Gershwin, Porter, Mancini, Brubeck — the standards that built the golden age of the piano bar, played with warmth and swing.

II

The Arabic Golden Age

Om Kalthoum, Fairuz, Abdel Halim Hafez — melodies beloved across the Arab world, re-imagined as elegant solo piano.

III

Andalusian Improvisation

Live improvisation in Hijaz and Rast maqam, rooted in Moroccan tradition — a sound no other lounge pianist can offer.
