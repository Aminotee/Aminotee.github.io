01 — About

Music for the room

Aminotee is a pianist playing for hotel lounges, bars, restaurants and private functions. The set list is built around what a room actually wants to hear while it eats, talks and relaxes: warm jazz standards, familiar sentimental pieces and smooth lounge material — plus Andalusian and Arabic-flavored pieces that most touring lounge pianists don't carry, giving venues something locally distinctive.

Alongside the standards, I compose original music — soft ambient pieces, jazz and blues — which means a venue can have its own sound rather than a playlist everyone has already heard.
